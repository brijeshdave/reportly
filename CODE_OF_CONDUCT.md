# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a good community:

- Being kind and patient with people, including people who are new.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and offering it generously.
- Focusing on what is best for the project rather than for ourselves.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at the address in the repository
profile. Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they
judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
